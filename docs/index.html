HostelLedger User Guide

Welcome to HostelLedger, the modern expense tracking app designed for shared living spaces. Whether you're sharing a hostel room, apartment, or planning a group trip, HostelLedger makes it easy to split bills, track debts, and settle up without the awkward conversations.

Group Expenses

Create groups for your roommates or friends and split bills evenly or by custom amounts.

Smart Wallet

Track your balance, see who owes you, and settle debts with a single tap.

1 Getting Started

Creating an Account

To start using HostelLedger, you need to create an account. Simply visit the signup page, enter your details, and verify your email.

  1. Open the app and click Sign Up.
  2. Enter your name, email, and choose a strong password.
  3. Click Create Account.
  4. Check your email for a verification link.

Note: We require email verification to ensure security and prevent spam accounts.

Signup Page

Logging In

Once verified, log in to access your dashboard.

Enter your email and password on the login screen. You can also use "Forgot Password" if you need to reset your credentials.

Login Page

2 Managing Groups

Groups are the core of HostelLedger. A group can be a specific event (e.g., "Weekend Trip"), a shared apartment ("Apt 4B"), or just a circle of friends.

Creating a Group

  • Go to the Groups tab.
  • Tap the + button to create a new group.
  • Give your group a name and an emoji (e.g., "Pizza Night 🍕").
  • Add members by their username (e.g., @john_doe) or invite them via email.

Joining a Group

If someone invites you, you'll receive a notification. You can accept the invitation from the Notifications tab or via the email link sent to you.

3 Tracking Expenses

Adding an Expense

When you pay for something shared, add it to the group so everyone knows their share.

1

Tap Add Expense on the dashboard or inside a group.

2

Enter the amount and a description (e.g., "Groceries").

3

Select who was involved. By default, it splits equally among all selected members.

4

Tap Save.

How Splits Work

HostelLedger uses "penny-perfect" splitting. If you split $10 among 3 people, it allocates $3.34 to one person and $3.33 to the others, ensuring the total always matches exactly.

4 Wallet & Settlements

Your Wallet

Your wallet represents your "real" money balance within the app context. You can manually add funds to your wallet record (this is just for tracking, it doesn't connect to a real bank).

When you pay for an expense, the amount is deducted from your wallet balance automatically.

Settling Debts

If you owe money to someone:

  1. Go to the group or your "To Pay" list.
  2. Tap Pay next to the person's name.
  3. Confirm the amount.
  4. The app will record the payment and update balances.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this connect to my real bank account?

No. HostelLedger is an expense tracker. The "Wallet" is a digital record for you to track your spending. You still need to make real-world payments via cash or bank apps.

Can I use it offline?

Yes! HostelLedger works offline. You can view your groups and add expenses. Everything will sync up once you're back online.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. We use Google Firebase for secure authentication and data storage. Only group members can see the expenses within their group.
