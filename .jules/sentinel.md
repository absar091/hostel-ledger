## 2024-06-10 - Missing Authentication Middleware on Critical Express Endpoints
**Vulnerability:** Several backend endpoints (/api/send-temp-member-alert, /api/send-welcome, /api/send-invitation, /api/send-external-invitation) were accessing req.user.uid or req.user.email without explicitly applying the authenticate middleware, leading to unhandled TypeErrors or potential unauthorized access if they were somehow reached.
**Learning:** In the Express backend, the authenticate middleware is not applied globally. Any endpoint that accesses req.user must explicitly include the authenticate middleware in its route definition to prevent authentication bypasses and unhandled TypeErrors.
**Prevention:** Always verify that authenticate is explicitly passed to any app.post() or app.get() route definition that expects req.user to be populated.
