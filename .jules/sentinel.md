## 2024-05-24 - Missing Input Validation on Email Endpoints
**Vulnerability:** The `/api/send-welcome` endpoint lacks proper input validation for the `email` field. It allows any string format, including potentially malicious payloads (e.g., `<script>alert(1)</script>@example.com`), which could lead to email header injection, Cross-Site Scripting (XSS) in email clients, or other unexpected behavior when processed by the email service provider.
**Learning:** Even internal or transactional endpoints sending emails must rigidly validate the email address format. Relying solely on external services to fail or filter bad inputs is not secure. A defense-in-depth approach mandates strict server-side validation.
**Prevention:** Implement strict email format validation across all endpoints that accept an email address using a robust regular expression or a dedicated validation library. E.g. `/^[^\s@<>"'\`]+@[^\s@<>"'\`]+\.[^\s@<>"'\`]+$/`.
