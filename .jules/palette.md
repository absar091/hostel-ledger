## 2024-05-20 - [Tooltip Pattern Learnings]
**Learning:** Native `title` attributes on buttons lead to inconsistent UX and accessibility issues. Radix Tooltips are preferred, but they must strictly be used with `<TooltipProvider>`. When replacing native titles in lists/maps, the key prop must be moved to the outermost `<Tooltip>` element, and global/parent `delayDuration={0}` is needed for instant tooltips.
**Action:** Replace `title` attributes on sidebar items with `<Tooltip>` from `@/components/ui/tooltip`, moving `key` and ensuring `<TooltipProvider delayDuration={0}>` is wrapped correctly.
