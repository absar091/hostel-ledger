## 2024-05-18 - Replacing Native Browser Tooltips with Custom Radix Tooltips
**Learning:** In interactive modal dialogs and dynamic UI components, native browser `title` attributes on buttons (like `title="Copy Reference"`) are slow to appear, inconsistent across devices, and offer poor accessibility. They can also clash visually if custom tooltip components are available but not consistently used.
**Action:** When working on tooltips for icon-only buttons (especially in contexts like `TransactionDetailModal` or `CreateGroupSheet` where space is tight and buttons are numerous), wrap the button in a `Tooltip` -> `TooltipTrigger asChild` and use `TooltipContent` to provide a fast, accessible, and stylistically consistent custom tooltip. Always remember to remove the native `title` attribute to avoid double-tooltips.
