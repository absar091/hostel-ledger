## 2025-03-09 - Radix Tooltip Providers & Native Tooltips
**Learning:** When injecting `<Tooltip>` wrappers around existing components programmatically using a script, replacing native `title="..."` attributes is important to prevent a "double-tooltip" effect. Additionally, ensure the `TooltipProvider` wraps the entire section, or use individual providers safely. Setting `delayDuration={0}` makes it more responsive.
**Action:** When adding custom tooltips, remember to specifically script the removal of generic `title` attributes on the elements being wrapped and verify the `z-index` of `<TooltipContent>` to ensure they aren't hidden behind sheets or dialogs.
