## $(date +%Y-%m-%d) - Added missing ARIA labels to GroupChat icon buttons
**Learning:** Found multiple icon-only buttons lacking `aria-label`s in the `GroupChat` component, making them inaccessible to screen readers. Specifically, state-dependent buttons (like hint toggles) required dynamic labels to accurately reflect their current state.
**Action:** When creating or reviewing components with icon-only interactive elements, verify that `aria-label` attributes are consistently applied, and ensure they update dynamically when representing togglable states (e.g., `aria-label={isOpen ? "Hide" : "Show"}`).
