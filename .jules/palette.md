## 2024-05-30 - Replace Native Title Attributes with Radix Tooltips
**Learning:** Native `title` attributes on interactive elements (like buttons or list items) present accessibility and usability issues. They don't appear consistently across all browsers, their display delay cannot be controlled, and they are generally ignored by screen readers or poorly handled on touch devices.
**Action:** Replace native `title` attributes with custom Tooltip components (like Radix UI `<Tooltip>`) to ensure consistent, accessible, and stylable feedback for micro-interactions across all platforms.
